Privacy policy

22 November 2026.

Who handles your data

The data controller is Greta Naselli, sole trader, Italian VAT no. IT06038500879, via Pace 65, 95018 Riposto (CT), Italy. For any question about your data, write to me from the Contact page.

What I collect and why

  • Newsletter. If you subscribe on the Italian site, I use your email to send the newsletter. The basis is your consent and you can unsubscribe from every email. It is managed with MailerLite (MailerLite Limited, Ireland).
  • Shop list. If you complete the Italian stockist form, I keep the shop's details in MailerLite to write to you when pieces are available.
  • Messages. The site's contact forms save nothing: they prepare an email that leaves from your own mailbox. I use what you write only to reply.
  • Purchases of handmade pieces (Italy only). I collect name, email, phone and shipping address to deliver the order and keep tax records. The basis is the contract and legal obligations.
  • PDF patterns. Payment takes place on Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland). I collect name, email and the time of your consent to immediate delivery, to deliver the file and keep legal and tax records. The file and purchase record are stored with Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.). To email your download link, your address joins a buyers' group in MailerLite: you don't receive the newsletter unless you subscribe. The basis is the contract and legal obligations.

Cookies and statistics

The site uses Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) to count visits, only if you accept it in the banner. Without consent it does not load. You can change your mind through "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage.

The site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which records visitors' IP addresses for security. Fonts are loaded from the site itself.

How long

I keep newsletter data while you are subscribed, messages for as long as needed to handle your request, and purchase data for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, limit its use or receive it in a readable format, and you can withdraw consent. You can complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or to the authority in the country where you live.
