Upcycled bags found nowhere else

Bags and accessories sewn by hand in Sicily from upholstery samples that companies discontinue at the end of a collection. No two are the same.

Tell me about your shop
The material

New fabrics, €30 to €180 per metre

Upholstery sample books are how companies present their collections. These are new, high-quality fabrics: at the end of the season they are discontinued and, unless someone saves them, thrown away.

I cut them, piece them together and sew them by hand in my studio in the Sicilian countryside. I work alone. There is no production line, not even a small one: each piece starts from the material in front of me, not from a model to repeat.
