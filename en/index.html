About me

I stitch together memory, beauty and the future

My name is Greta Naselli and I founded Repunto. I am a textile artist, sustainable fashion consultant and teacher of zero-waste sewing.

I studied Fashion Techniques and Cultures at the University of Bologna's Rimini campus, and pattern making at Secoli Next Fashion School in Bologna. Since 2022 I have led the upcycling workshop “Fashion, theatre, sustainability” at Teatro Duse in Bologna.

I live in the Sicilian countryside, where I grow my vision between soil, threads and stories: slow gestures, transformed garments, permaculture and natural farming.

Questions

Before you write to me

Where do the fabrics come from?

They are upholstery samples: new fabrics that companies use to present their collections. When a collection changes, the samples are discontinued. I save them and turn them into unique pieces.

Do you ship outside Italy?

The online shop ships within Italy only. I ship to shops and stockists across Europe.

Where is the studio?

In the countryside at the foot of Mount Etna, near Regalbuto, in Sicily, Italy.
